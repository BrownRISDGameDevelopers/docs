The Brown RISD Game Developers is a joint club run between [Brown University](https://brown.edu) and [the Rhode Island School of Design](https://risd.edu) (RISD). Our club operates as an indie game development studio, focused on creating collaborative, student-run games.

Any member of our club can pitch a game idea at the beginning of each semester. The entire club will then vote on 3-4 games they'd like to see developed, and we spend the entire semester making them come to life!

Each game is led by a Producer, who will be in charge of a team of artists, programmers, sound engineers, and writers.
